# tavya vs Manus

How tavya differs from Manus, the general-purpose agent, as of November 2026, and when to choose which.

## What Manus is

Manus is a general agent that "plans the task, calls tools, and runs work in its execution environments": research, slides, video, websites and errands in a cloud browser. Wide Research starts hundreds of agents on one question. Automations start it from schedules, email, calendars, webhooks and other apps. Cue, in early access, gives each personal agent its own "email, phone number, wallet, and computer". Manus uses its own agent and sells credits: free, or from $20 a month.

## What is the same

Agents with a cloud computer, a browser that stays signed in, an address for mail, work that fans out to many agents, schedules and events, and results that are not code.

## Where Manus is ahead

- **Polish for everyday tasks**: slides, video and websites are built-in products.
- **Phone**: a mobile app, and with Cue a phone number for the agent.
- **Scale of one job**: hundreds of research agents from one request.

## Where tavya is different

- **Your choice of agent and model.** A task runs Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan or API key. Manus runs its own agent on its credits.
- **Built around real projects.** Git repositories, pull requests, a merge queue, large data with versions, project secrets: an agent works where your work lives.
- **Review you can set.** Each task passes a review route of people and agents before it lands; one agent can check another's work ([Let one agent review another](/docs/agent-review)).
- **Teams.** People and agents share one set of permissions.
- **Your own cloud account**: E2B, Daytona, Fly.io, Hetzner, AWS or Google Cloud.
- **Open source**, under the GNU AGPL-3.0.

## Which to choose

Choose Manus when the result is a deck, a video or a piece of research and you want it in one request. Choose tavya when the work belongs to a project that lasts (a codebase, a paper, a business) and you want agents of your choice doing it under review.
